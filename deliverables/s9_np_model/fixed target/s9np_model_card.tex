\section{Task and robot}
A Scout~V2 mobile base with a 6-DOF Doosan arm must bring its \emph{gripper frame} coincident
(\emph{confondu} --- position AND orientation) with a \textbf{fixed} target frame. This is the
direct-velocity baseline (agent \code{ScoutsanV1\_1b}): the policy commands base and joint
velocities \emph{directly}, in contrast to the WDLS/Path~B model that emits WDLS weights. This run
(\code{s9\_np}) is re-based onto the wb Path~B physics so it can be compared to the WDLS models on
matched dynamics. The target is a single fixed pose (no randomization); a whole-body-transplant
warm start (padded ARM 14/6 $\to$ 14/8) seeds the arm skill while the grippy 4-wheel drive is
learned fresh.

\section{Actions (8) --- direct velocity}
Six arm-joint velocity commands (deltas clamped to one \code{maxJointVelocityDeg} step per decision,
cap 30\,\textdegree/s) plus base $v,\omega$ (scaled by \code{maxLinearVel}=0.3, \code{maxAngularVel}=0.4).
Base $\to$ wheels by differential drive (wheelRadius 0.16459, wheelSeparation 0.58306), each wheel's
commanded velocity clamped to \code{maxWheelVelocityDeg}=150\,\textdegree/s (wb-matched). The base
drive + clamps are identical to the WDLS agents; only the \emph{source} of $v,\omega$ differs
(direct policy vs WDLS solve).

\section{Reward and gate}
Per step: a progress term $r_1$, orientation/position shaping, directional ($\pm$) terms that are
\emph{zeroed inside the gate} (deadbands, so stillness at the goal is optimal), and a rate penalty
$r_8$; a one-time \textbf{+100} bonus on success. Exact constants/equations: the s9 stage report.

FAIL    & collision/fall (early exit) & penalty, end\\
