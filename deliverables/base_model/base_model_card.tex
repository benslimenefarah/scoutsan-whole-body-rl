\section{Task and robot}
A Scout~V2 differential-drive base must reach a target position on the flat arena and
\emph{stop inside the success gate}. This is the first stage of the project lineage
(\code{bearing\_angle\_FLU}); the model documented here is a \textbf{from-scratch retrain on the
current physics}. One decision = one policy step; the episode ends on success, on a fall, or at
the step cap. The agent script is \code{ScoutsanAgent\_4obs\_1} (the ``FLU-native'' variant): the
Unity$\leftrightarrow$ROS handedness lives entirely inside the agent, so the policy operates in a
single (FLU) convention and needs no sign flips at inference.

%% =====================================================================
\section{Observations (4)}
Emitted in the robot-local FLU frame (forward-left-up), computed from the target in the base's
local frame:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cll}
\hline
\# & Symbol & Definition (FLU, robot frame)\\
\hline
0 & \code{forwardDist} & $+z_{\text{local}}$ to target (FLU $x$, forward)\\
1 & \code{lateralDist} & $-x_{\text{local}}$ to target (FLU $y$, left)\\
2 & \code{linVelForward} & forward component of base linear velocity\\
3 & \code{angVel} & $-\omega_y$, yaw rate (FLU CCW-positive)\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Range and bearing are recovered from the first two:
$d=\sqrt{\text{forwardDist}^2+\text{lateralDist}^2}$, \;
$\beta=\operatorname{atan2}(\text{lateralDist},\,\text{forwardDist})$ \; ($\beta=0$ is dead ahead).

%% =====================================================================
\section{Actions (2) and drive}
Two continuous actions in $[-1,1]$, in FLU convention:
$a_0=v_{\text{ros}}$ (forward, m/s), $a_1=w_{\text{ros}}$ (yaw, rad/s, CCW-positive).
They are converted to Unity ($v=v_{\text{ros}},\; w=-w_{\text{ros}}$) and mapped to the four
wheels by differential drive with half-track $L/2$:
\[
v_{\text{left}}=\frac{v-w\,L/2}{r},\qquad
v_{\text{right}}=\frac{v+w\,L/2}{r},\qquad
r=0.16459\text{ m},\; L=0.58306\text{ m}.
\]
Left wheels take $-v_{\text{left}}$, right wheels $+v_{\text{right}}$ (URDF wheel-axis sign).

\section{Reward}
Per step, three shaping terms plus terminal bonuses:
\[
r_t \;=\; \underbrace{0.1\,(d_{t-1}-d_t)}_{\text{progress}}
       \;-\; \underbrace{0.001}_{\text{time}}
       \;-\; \underbrace{0.005\,\beta_t\,f(d_t)}_{\text{bearing}} .
\]
$\beta_t\ge0$ is the angle between the target direction and the \emph{nearer} robot axis (front if
the target is ahead, rear if behind). The bearing term \emph{fades out} near the goal so it does
not fight the stop:
\[
f(d)=\operatorname{clamp}_{[0,1]}\!\left(\frac{d-0.8}{1.8-0.8}\right)
\quad(\text{full beyond }1.8\text{ m, off below }0.8\text{ m}).
\]
Terminal (override the step reward): $+1$ on success, $-1$ on fall.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\hline
Constant & Value & Meaning\\
\hline
\code{progressCoeff} & 0.1 & reward per metre of distance closed\\
\code{timePenalty} & 0.001 & per-step cost (encourages promptness)\\
\code{bearingCoeff} & 0.005 & alignment penalty weight\\
\code{bearingFadeStart / End} & 1.8 / 0.8 m & fade band of the bearing term\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

%% =====================================================================
\section{Gates and episode ends}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\hline
Event & Condition & Reward\\
\hline
SUCCESS & $d<\code{successRadius}=0.6$ m & $+1$, end episode\\
FALL & $y<\code{fallY}=-1.0$ m & $-1$, end episode\\
TIMEOUT & step cap reached & shaping only\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Spawn: the target is placed uniformly in a $\pm5.0$~m square around the base start, re-sampled
until at least \code{minSpawnDistance}$=1.5$~m away (target can appear behind the robot).
